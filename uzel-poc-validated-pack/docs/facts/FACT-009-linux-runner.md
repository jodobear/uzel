- **Claim:** The pinned portable nampplets runtime can verify, launch, and route a signed self-contained napplet in a Linux Tauri/WebKit host without child native authority.
- **Source revision:** initially validated at `jodobear/nampplets@08ddb87a975dcc44c8826e4c9c7fa7cfe7f701bf`; current validated Rust runtime `jodobear/nampplets@d533a63d519c14470f900323958509cdea1c6479`, transitively retaining `pablof7z/nmp@005dc2a5f12aa414961b313d05ebb021934e385c`; portable shell assets come from contribution head `fc68bce0a4793a8618445e234bcc91d69e8b96de`.

- **Observed result:** The signed `good-morning` fixture verified to aggregate `828a6df0...`, launched with `identity`, `inc`, `outbox`, `resource`, and `shell`, completed `shell.init`, and answered `identity.getPublicKey`. `link` and `theme` remain unavailable. An unknown shell-owned surface was rejected, forged session/principal fields did not authorize routing, and the sandboxed child had no Tauri globals, Wry IPC function, or readable parent. Linux builds and browser smokes passed.
- **Decision:** Reuse the upstream controller and trusted shell unchanged. Compose the Linux NAP-RESOURCE provider through the upstream Rust-provider seam; do not manufacture `link` or `theme` grants. Slice 02 temporarily composed the controller inside Tauri; Slice 04 completed the bounded transfer and moved the controller to the daemon. The version-0 frame ceiling remains 524288 bytes. Inbound envelopes remain limited to 65536 bytes; larger routed responses created by NAP-RESOURCE cross the same connection in bounded chunks up to the trusted shell's aggregate transport ceiling.
